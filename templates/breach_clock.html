Breach clock

Alarm | Incident report | Incident records

This is a reminder, not legal advice. A person decides whether notification is required. This screen never decides, reports or sends anything.

Assess within 30 calendar days of credible grounds to believe a breach occurred. Notify the PDPC as soon as practicable, no later than 3 calendar days after the human determination that it is notifiable.

Affected people, where notification is required, must be notified as soon as practicable, at the same time as or after the PDPC. Counts and harm facts do not start a clock or decide notification.

Asia/Singapore calendar dates, not rolling hours. Day one is the next date; the ceiling is the end of the deadline date. Refresh to update. As of {{ as_of }}.
