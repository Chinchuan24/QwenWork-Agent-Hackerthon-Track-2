**Title:** Twinguard: Keep Control Of Your AI Helpers

**One sentence:** Twinguard is a local prototype that checks AI helpers' actions and numbers against business records, holds or blocks risky work, and prepares a plain owner briefing.

**Project description (under 300 words):**

Running a business alone should not mean trusting every AI-written email or checking every file by hand. Twinguard is a local prototype for checking what AI helpers try to do before their actions take effect.

Our demonstration uses Lumen Studio, a fictional one-person design business. Every person, company, amount, identifier and record in that world is invented. Emails, uploads, payments and deletions are simulated; no real business action is carried out.

The prototype records attempted work, checks task limits and business rules, and compares claims with source files. In a saved scripted rehearsal, it flags three wrong figures in a client reply and holds the message. It also records a blocked client-list upload attempt. A morning note is derived from stored records so the owner can review what needs attention.

Aegis and Delta are the two guards in the product design. Saved stand-in checks include separate answers and held disagreements, but coverage of every outside action is not yet proven.

QwenWork has supported development and review. Current rehearsals use clearly labelled, rule-based stand-ins. Live model paths are ready for key access but have not been run; a working QwenWork worker connection and morning schedule still need proof.

The prototype is not yet release-ready. Ordinary-task false holds, exact sponsor outcomes, browser proof navigation and live deployment remain open. The briefing video and demo recording are pending. Website copy is prepared, but a verified Hashmeta AI build remains an operator handoff. We present the saved evidence and the gaps, not a claim of complete protection.
